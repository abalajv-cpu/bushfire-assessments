---
title: "Can I reduce my BAL rating?"
description: "Four practical ways a Bushfire Attack Level can sometimes be lowered, and when it cannot."
date: 2026-11-02
category: "BAL ratings"
---

A BAL rating is the product of vegetation, distance and slope. You cannot change the slope, but the other inputs are sometimes within your control.

## Options worth looking at

- Move the building: a few metres of extra separation can drop a rating by a level.
- Establish an asset protection zone: managed, low-fuel land between the building and the bush counts as separation, where clearing is lawful.
- Check the vegetation classification: woodland, forest, scrub and grassland are assessed differently, and state mapping is not always right.
- Use shielding: under AS 3959, an elevation not exposed to the hazard can sometimes be built to the next lower level.

## When it cannot be reduced

If the hazard is on neighbouring land you do not control, or clearing is restricted by vegetation or environmental rules, the rating may be fixed. In that case the focus moves to building well at the rating you have.
