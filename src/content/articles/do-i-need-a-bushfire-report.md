title: "Do I need a bushfire report for my development application?"

If your land is mapped as bushfire prone, the answer is almost always yes. The mapping, and the type of report, differ between Queensland and New South Wales.

## Queensland

Check the bushfire hazard overlay in your council planning scheme mapping. If the lot is inside the overlay, development applications are assessed against the bushfire overlay code, and council will usually expect a site-specific bushfire hazard assessment. For building work, your certifier will want a BAL rating.

## New South Wales

Check whether the land is on the bush fire prone land map. It is noted on the section 10.7 planning certificate, and the NSW Rural Fire Service has an online lookup tool. On mapped land, a development application for a dwelling needs a bush fire assessment report showing compliance with Planning for Bush Fire Protection 2019.

Subdivisions and special fire protection purpose developments, such as childcare, schools, aged care and tourist accommodation, need a Bush Fire Safety Authority from the RFS.
